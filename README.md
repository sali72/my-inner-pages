# 📓 Meet Yourself on the Page

> An AI-powered journaling space for deep self-knowledge and inner clarity.

---

## ✨ What Is This?

**Meet Yourself on the Page** is a private journaling app where AI acts as a gentle mirror — not a chatbot, not a therapist. It reads your entries and reflects back the patterns, feelings, and tendencies you might not notice on your own.

Write freely. See yourself more clearly. Grow.

---

## 🪄 How It Works

1. **✍️ Write** — Pour your thoughts onto the page. No judgment, no pressure.
2. **🔍 Reflect** — The AI offers gentle reflections based on what you've written.
3. **💬 Chat** — Have a real-time conversation with the AI about your journal,
   patterns, or anything on your mind. Chats are saved and browsable.
4. **💡 Understand** — Spot patterns, make connections, and gain real clarity over time.

### The Four Reflection Lenses

- 🌊 **Emotional** — What am I feeling?
- 🧠 **Cognitive** — What am I believing?
- 🔄 **Behavioral** — What am I doing?
- 🤝 **Relational** — How do I relate to others?

---

## 🔒 Your Privacy Matters

Your journal is yours alone. Entries are protected behind secure authentication, never shared, never exposed. Just you and your words.

---

## 💛 Who Is This For?

- Reflective souls seeking self-knowledge
- People working on personal growth
- Those using journaling alongside therapy
- Overthinkers craving clarity
- Anyone who just needs a quiet place to think

---

## ⚠️ A Small but Important Note

This is a self-reflection tool, not a mental health product. If you're going through something serious, please reach out to a qualified professional. 💙
